# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), confirmed by the user to match the current merajourney.com deployment. Motion stack delegated: Three.js for real-time 3D, GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis for scroll choreography.

## Users

Travelers planning a private trip to Nepal or Bhutan: trekkers, climbers (Mera Peak, expedition-style), culture seekers, wildlife lovers, couples, and comfort-paced travelers. Parties of 1–12. They arrive wanting someone they can trust on the ground and leave by booking a free 30-minute discovery call or sending an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Mera Journey is a personal travel advisory for Nepal & Bhutan, run by Kai Mai (Philadelphia, PA; Meratech LLC). Kai designs custom private itineraries and handles hotels, trek permits (TIMS, conservation areas, restricted regions), Bhutan visa/permits, guide matching, and 24/7 WhatsApp support during the trip. Expedition advisory covers route planning, team building, helicopter evacuation insurance guidance, and altitude fitness preparation. Success: a visitor books a discovery call.

## Positioning

Not a catalog and not a mass-market tour operator. Kai spent four months in Nepal, climbed Mera Peak, filmed a documentary on mad honey hunters, lived in hill villages, and works directly with local operators he knows by name — no middlemen. The site's truth is one person's lived journey, not a company's fleet of departures.

## Operating Context

Process: 1) Discovery call (30 min, Calendly https://calendly.com/kaimaifoto/30min) 2) Custom plan 3) Refine together (routes, permits, guides, logistics) 4) Go, with vetted locals and Kai's support before and during the trip.

Destinations: Nepal ("The Roof of the World"; Everest, Annapurna, Langtang, remote valleys; Mera Peak; Chitwan & Bardia; Kathmandu Valley; mad honey hunting; eight of the fourteen 8,000 m peaks; best Mar–May, Sep–Nov). Bhutan ("The Last Shangri-La"; Tiger's Nest, Druk Path, Jomolhari; festivals and dzongs; comfort-paced programs; combined Nepal + Bhutan; Sustainable Development Fee; best Mar–May, Sep–Nov).

1. Lived, not listed: every claim traces back to Kai's own time on the ground.
2. One person you can call: the path to a human conversation is always one step away.
